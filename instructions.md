# Instructions

How to Change Oil on a 10th Generation Honda Civic (2016-2021) 
Step 1: Park the car on a flat surface. 
Step 2: Press the lever inside the vehicle around underneath the wheel. This will push your 
hood up. Move the lever under the hood around the middle of the car, roughly above the 
emblem, and pop open the hood.  
Step 3: Lift the Vehicle in a Safe Manner.  
Step 4: Place a car jack under the front jack point. (
Step 5: Jack up the car, usually by pushing down on the lever 
) 
Step 6:  Position the jack stands under the vehicle frame(usually around the jacking points, 
metal and base part of the car, not the plastic) 
Step 7: Lower the car onto the jack stands. 
Step 8: Locate the Oil Drain Bolt ( bottom of the engine oil pan, usually facing toward the 
rear end of the car) 
Step 9: Slide the oil drain pan under the engine. (available in Walmart, AutoZone, and any 
auto shop) 
Step 10: Position the drain pan directly beneath the bolt. 
Step 11: Drain the Old Oil 
Step 12: Loosen the oil drain bolt with the socket wrench. (can be loosened clockwise)  
Step 13: Wait until the old oil is drained from the oil pan. 
Step 14: Replace the Oil Filter(
) 
Step 15:Loosen and remove the oil filter using an oil filter wrench(can be loosened 
clockwise) 
Step 16: With your hands, add oil around the new filter to make it easier to insert, then 
install it.  
Step 17: Reinstall the Drain Bolt (Insert the drain bolt back into the oil pan) 
Step 18: Tighten the bolt with a socket wrench(counterclockwise)  
Step 19: Add New Engine Oil 
Step 20: Remove the oil cap on top of the engine. 
Step 21: Insert a funnel into the oil fill hole.(
) 
Step 22: Pour the suggested amount of oil into the engine.(3.7 quarts for the 1.5L Turbo 
engine or 4.4  quarts for the 2.0L naturally aspirated engine. Use 0W-20 full synthetic oil.) 
Step 23: Check the Oil Level. 
Step 24: Start the engine for a few seconds. 
Step 25: Turn the engine off. 
Step 26: Remove the oil dipstick. (under the hood, identifiable as an often yellow or orange 
ring or T-shaped handle) 
Step 27: Clean the dipstick with a clean rag. (
Step 28: Insert the dipstick back into the engine. 
Step 29: Pull the dipstick out again to check the oil level. 
Step 30: Read oil level, color should be of brand new oil and at full line) 
Step 31: Update oil change in the Car System. 
Step 32: Tap Vehicle Settings on the display screen. 
Step 33: Tap Maintenance Information from the menu. 
Step 34: Tap Reset on the oil life screen. 
Step 35: In the pop-up, tap confirm, then the system will update.
